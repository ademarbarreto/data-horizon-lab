# Data hygiene
Data hygiene (higienização de dados) is the process of cleaning, correcting, and standardizing data in a database to ensure it remains accurate, complete, and up to date.Key BenefitsBetter Decisions: Prevents errors caused by outdated or faulty records.Higher Productivity: Saves time for teams working with customer relationship management (CRM) or analytics tools.No Duplicates: Removes repeated entries to keep storage and lists clean.Common PracticesStandardize Formats: Fix phone numbers, addresses, and text casing.Fill Missing Info: Complete empty fields for customer names or corporate IDs.Remove Errors: Delete or fix corrupted, irrelevant, or fake entries.
